The AQD Method™
The Origin

Built by a journeyman electrician from Pittsburgh.

My name is Abdul Qawwee Daniels. I am not a Silicon Valley engineer. I came up through the trades — 15 years in construction electrical, a journeyman ticket, IBEW Local 5 Pittsburgh. I know how things are built. I know what breaks. And I know how to document it.

I noticed that nobody was automatically connecting environmental triggers to physiological responses in real time — with legal rigor, with tamper-evident records, with clinical output ready for EHR systems and insurance billing. Doctors had health records. Facilities had sensor logs. Nobody was automating the connection between them.

So I built the system that does it. Then I built three more layers on top of it. Then I built two intelligent safety hardware inventions on top of that. Then I filed all five at the USPTO — on my own, no attorney — and got to work on the prototype.

"Four patents. One problem. Filed on the same day. No attorney. $80 per application."

The prototype is operational. A live environmental event has been detected, timestamped, and validated across five simultaneous sensor channels. The patent clock is running. Now I am looking for the right partners to take this to market.
