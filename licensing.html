What is CEHE?

CEHE (Causal Environmental Health Event System) is a multi-patent environmental health intelligence platform that combines distributed sensing, predictive analytics, and autonomous monitoring to provide real-time insights into environmental health conditions.

Unlike traditional air quality monitors that measure pollutant concentrations, CEHE focuses on causal relationships between environmental conditions and human health outcomes, enabling organizations to make data-driven decisions that protect people and assets.

Key Differentiators

  • Causal health event identification (not just pollution levels)
  • Predictive forecasting with machine learning
  • Distributed autonomous monitoring network
  • Real-time actionable intelligence
  • Scalable across industries and geographies
